Privacy policy

Toolbox Uploader · last updated 24 November 2026

Toolbox Uploader is a personal command-line tool. Its owner runs it on their own computer to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what it does with Google and TikTok account data.

Data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and grants access, the tool uses the YouTube Data API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube) to:

  • upload video files and set their title, description, tags, category, privacy, language and scheduled publish time;
  • read the signed-in channel's ID, title and handle, to confirm uploads go to the intended channel;
  • read the status of videos it uploaded.

When the owner signs in with TikTok and grants access, the tool uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish) to:

  • upload original video files as Inbox drafts or Direct Posts and set post title, caption, and privacy settings;
  • read the signed-in account's basic profile (display name and avatar), to confirm uploads go to the intended account;
  • check the publish status of videos it uploaded.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform these actions for the owner. It is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling, and it is not used to train any model.

Storage

Google and TikTok sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in its local configuration. The tool runs no server and keeps no database. Video files and their details stay on the owner's computer until the owner uploads them to YouTube or TikTok.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The only services the tool talks to are Google and TikTok (when authorized for each platform), and only to perform the actions above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Toolbox Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. TikTok access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions. Deleting the token from the computer's configuration also removes the tool's access.
