Privacy policy

Toolbox Uploader · last updated 6 November 2026

Toolbox Uploader is a personal command-line tool. Its owner runs it on their own computer to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels. This policy explains what it does with Google account data.

Data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and grants access, the tool uses the YouTube Data API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube) to:

  • upload video files and set their title, description, tags, category, privacy, language and scheduled publish time;
  • read the signed-in channel's ID, title and handle, to confirm uploads go to the intended channel;
  • read the status of videos it uploaded.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform these actions for the owner. It is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling, and it is not used to train any model.

Storage

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in its local configuration. The tool runs no server and keeps no database. Video files and their details stay on the owner's computer until the owner uploads them to YouTube.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The only service the tool talks to is Google, and only to perform the actions above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Toolbox Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token from the computer's configuration also removes the tool's access.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through NaviRamyle on GitHub.
