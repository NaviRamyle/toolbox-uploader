Toolbox Uploader

A personal tool for publishing my own videos to my own YouTube channels.

What it does

Toolbox Uploader is a small command-line program that I run on my own computer. With my permission, given through Google sign-in, it:

  • uploads videos I made to my own YouTube channels;
  • sets each video's title, description, tags, privacy and scheduled publish time;
  • reads my channel's name and handle, to make sure a video goes to the right channel;
  • checks the status of videos it uploaded.

Who it is for

It is not a public service and there is nothing to sign up for. It is used only by its owner, on channels the owner manages.

Your data

Toolbox Uploader does not collect, sell or share anyone's data. Read the privacy policy for the details.
