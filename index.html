Toolbox Uploader

A personal tool for publishing my own videos to my own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts.

What it does

Toolbox Uploader is a small command-line program that I run on my own computer. With my permission, given through Google or TikTok sign-in, it:

  • uploads videos I made to my own YouTube channels (via the YouTube Data API) and TikTok accounts (via the TikTok Content Posting API);
  • sets each video's title, description, tags, privacy and scheduled publish time;
  • reads my channel or account's name and handle, to make sure a video goes to the right destination;
  • checks the status of videos it uploaded.

Who it is for

It is not a public service and there is nothing to sign up for. It is used only by its owner, on channels and accounts the owner manages.

Your data

Toolbox Uploader does not collect, sell or share anyone's data. Read the privacy policy and terms of service for the details.
